I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Weichao Li and Prof. Jinbei Zhang. I also work as a research assistant at Peng Cheng Laboratory.

My research focuses on efficient and reliable network measurement, GPU-accelerated parallel computing, and wireless physical-layer systems. I am especially interested in turning algorithmic ideas into practical, low-latency systems.

Research Projects

GPU-Accelerated Massive MIMO Detection

Designed a Neumann-series approximate inverse and a hierarchical CUDA execution framework to reduce detection latency and improve throughput.

5D Wireless Channel Estimation

Implemented GPU-accelerated SVD and Lanczos methods for high-dimensional channel parameters including AoD, AoA, and propagation delay, reaching 10 ms-level processing.

Data-Center Network Monitoring

Built LMon around controllable ECMP probing, Lasso regression, and hypothesis testing to identify potential network failures with low monitoring overhead.

Embedded Intelligent Vehicles

Developed a CMOS-camera line-following vehicle with PID control and a 555-timer/H-bridge wireless-charging vehicle during undergraduate research.
