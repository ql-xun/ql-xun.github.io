About me关于我

Qinglin Xun 荀清林

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Weichao Li and Prof. Jinbei Zhang. I also work as a research assistant at Pengcheng Laboratory.

My research focuses on efficient and reliable network measurement, GPU-accelerated parallel computing, and wireless physical-layer systems. I am especially interested in turning algorithmic ideas into practical, low-latency systems.

我是中山大学信息与通信工程专业博士研究生，导师为李伟超教授和张金钡教授。我同时在鹏城实验室担任研究助理。

我的研究聚焦于高效可靠的网络测量、GPU 加速并行计算及无线物理层系统，尤其关注如何将算法思想转化为实用的低时延系统。

Research Projects研究项目

Selected systems and research directions.部分研究方向与系统项目。

GPU-Accelerated Massive MIMO DetectionGPU 加速大规模 MIMO 检测

Designed a Neumann-series approximate inverse and a hierarchical CUDA execution framework to reduce detection latency and improve throughput.设计 Neumann 级数近似求逆与层次化 CUDA 执行框架，以降低检测时延并提高吞吐率。

5D Wireless Channel Estimation五维无线信道估计

Implemented GPU-accelerated SVD and Lanczos methods for high-dimensional channel parameters, reaching 10 ms-level processing.面向高维信道参数实现 GPU 加速的 SVD 与 Lanczos 方法，达到 10 毫秒级处理时延。

Data-Center Network Monitoring数据中心网络监测

Built LMon around controllable ECMP probing, Lasso regression, and hypothesis testing to identify network failures with low monitoring overhead.基于可控 ECMP 探测、Lasso 回归和假设检验构建 LMon，以较低监测开销识别潜在网络故障。

Embedded Intelligent Vehicles嵌入式智能车

Developed a CMOS-camera line-following vehicle with PID control and a wireless-charging vehicle during undergraduate research.本科期间开发基于 CMOS 摄像头与 PID 控制的循迹车，以及无线充电智能车。
