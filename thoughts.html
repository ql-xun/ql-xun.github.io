Thoughts思考

Over the years, I have learned a great deal from people I admire and from experiences in my own research and life. I hope the notes collected here may help others, just as similar reflections have helped me. They are not prescriptions or formal advice, but small observations and lessons that I want to remember and share.

This page is inspired by Behnaz Arzani's Thoughts and Advice page, which helped me think about how to grow as a researcher and collaborator. For now, this will remain an informal and evolving collection rather than a structured column, and I hope to refine it over time.

这些年来，我从敬佩的人身上以及自己的科研与生活经历中学到了许多。我希望记录在这里的片段也能给他人带来帮助，就像类似的思考曾经帮助过我一样。这里并不是一套正式的建议或准则，而是一些我想记住并分享的观察与体会。

这个页面受到 Behnaz Arzani 的 Thoughts and Advice 页面启发；其中的内容促使我思考如何成为一名更好的研究者与合作者。现阶段，这里会保持为一个非结构化、持续更新的记录，我也会在未来逐步补充和完善。
